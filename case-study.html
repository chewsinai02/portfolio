MAIL · PDF · AI · MYCEIDS PUBLIC

AI Mail & Document Pipeline (MyCeids)

End-to-end for teams and bundles: documents enter either from email attachments (PHP email_receiver_system) or from manual upload—not both required. Supported inputs include ZIP and Office/images; the pipeline unpacks where needed, converts to PDF, and can compress large PDFs before Gemini classifies and extracts fields. Node + Playwright (headless Chromium) then runs the MyCeids upload steps on the server. Vite + React UI for review and hand-off.
